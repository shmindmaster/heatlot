**Required tests:** chemistry, tensile, hardness.

**Required statements:** heat number of the material supplied; specification and revision; melted and manufactured domestically; free of mercury contamination; a signed statement of conformance carrying the signatory's name and title.

**Verdicts, as defined for this inspection**

- **ACCEPT** — every requirement is met.
- **NONCONFORMING** — a reported value is outside the specification, or the specification revision is wrong.
- **INCOMPLETE** — something required is missing or cannot be traced: a test, a statement, or the heat link.

**How the documents were read.** Every page of all twelve PDFs was read. The text layer was extracted and the page objects were inspected separately for annotations, embedded images, form fields, and text drawn in white or at very small size, so that nothing hidden behind the visible page was missed. Exactly one such item exists, in PKT-31006; it is reported there and in [04-notes.md](04-notes.md). Values were compared against the limits both by hand and by script.

**Line references.** `Lnn` means the *nn*-th printed text line on page 1 of that document, counting only visible lines. All twelve documents are one page, so no page number is given. The numbering is described against the visible page, so it can be checked by eye.

**What was not checked.** The drawing LPM-7714 Rev D is called out on the PO but appears on none of the twelve documents, so drawing conformance is outside what this paperwork can establish. Signature authority, mill accreditation and certificate authenticity were not verified.
